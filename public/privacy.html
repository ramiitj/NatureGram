Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

NatureGram (thenaturegram.com and the NatureGram apps) is a field guide and community for people who love nature. It is operated by Benkut Private Limited, a company incorporated in India ("NatureGram", "we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why, how long we keep it, who we share it with, and the rights you have. We wrote it to meet India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and Information Technology Rules, the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and US state privacy laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA).

1. Who is responsible for your data

Benkut Private Limited is the data fiduciary (under Indian law) and data controller (under the GDPR) for personal data processed through NatureGram.

Grievance Officer and privacy contact: Roshini Ganuthula, roshiniganuthula@gmail.com. See section 12 for how complaints are handled.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: email address (or the Google account you sign in with), username, and the optional profile photo, bio and naturalist interests you add. If you explore as a guest, we create an anonymous account identifier.
  • What you share: photos, videos, sound recordings, species names, captions, field notes, comments, identifications, marvels (likes), follows, saved sightings and reports.
  • Letters: the messages you send to other explorers. See section 6 for who can read them.
  • Location: only if you allow it. Your device location is used to name the approximate area of a sighting (for example "Kaziranga, Assam"). Exact coordinates are never shown to anyone else. They are stored privately in your account for your own records. You can hide the area on any sighting, and the area is always hidden for protected species.
  • Live guide sessions: when you use the live lens, camera frames and microphone audio stream to our AI provider so the guide can answer. These streams are not recorded or stored by us.
  • Usage and security records: how many identifications, live minutes and shares you use each day (to apply the daily allowance), a record of each AI request without its content (time, type, size, outcome, network address), error reports, and, if you allow it, usage statistics (screens used, crashes, speed).
  • Device and push details: if you turn on notifications, a push token for your device.

We do not collect government identifiers, payment details or biometric templates, and we do not use device fingerprinting.

3. Why we use it, and on what basis

  • To provide NatureGram (contract / your request): your account, the feed and journal, identification, the live guide, letters and notifications you ask for.
  • To keep the community and wildlife safe (legitimate interests; legal obligation where applicable): checking photos before they are shared for people, faces, number plates and screens, filtering links and spam, applying the same daily allowance to everyone, reviewing reports, and preventing abuse.
  • To improve identification (legitimate interests): the corrections explorers make to suggested names, and public sightings, may be used to evaluate and improve how well identification works. You can object at any time by writing to the Grievance Officer; we will stop using your content for this.
  • Usage statistics and ad measurement (consent): only if you choose "Allow" in the app. You can change your mind at any time by clearing the choice in your browser or app settings.

We do not sell or rent your personal data, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

4. AI processing

NatureGram uses Google's Gemini models to suggest species names, describe behaviour, run the live guide and check photos before they are shared. The media and text needed for each request are sent to Google for processing under Google's terms for its API services. Names and notes are suggestions that can be wrong; you review and approve them before anything is published. Never eat, touch or handle a wild organism, or treat anyone, based on an identification.

5. Who we share it with

  • Other explorers: sightings you share to the feed, your profile (username, photo, bio, public sightings, follower counts), and your comments and identifications on public sightings.
  • Service providers who process data for us under contract: Google Cloud and Firebase (hosting, sign-in, database, file storage, notifications), Google Gemini API (AI features), and, only with your consent, Google Ads measurement. To name a sighting's area we send approximate coordinates to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service; to show species background we query Wikipedia with the species name.
  • Legal and safety reasons: when required by law or a valid legal order, or to protect people, wildlife or the service from harm.
  • Business changes: if NatureGram is reorganised or transferred, with this policy continuing to apply.

6. Letters are private

Letters are visible only to the two people in the conversation. NatureGram staff cannot browse them. If one of the participants reports a conversation, our administrator can read that conversation to investigate, and each such reading is recorded.

7. International transfers

Our providers store and process data in data centres in India, the United States, the European Union and other countries. Where data leaves the EU, UK or India, we rely on safeguards recognised by the applicable law, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses included in our providers' terms.

8. How long we keep it

  • Account, sightings, comments and letters: until you delete them or your account.
  • Daily allowance counters: about 3 days. Records of AI requests (without content): 30 days. The guide's suggestions kept to show on a sighting: about 3 days unless published.
  • Reports, moderation decisions and administrator actions: as long as needed to keep the community safe and meet legal obligations (generally up to 3 years), then deleted.
  • Backups: overwritten on a rolling basis.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to: access your data; correct or update it; delete it; receive a copy in a portable format; object to or restrict certain processing; withdraw consent; nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated (India); and complain to a supervisory authority (for example the Data Protection Board of India, or your EU/UK data protection authority). California residents may also request to know, delete and correct, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

  • Download your data: Profile → Settings → Download my data.
  • Delete your account: Profile → Settings → Delete account. This removes your profile, sightings, comments, marvels, follows and uploaded media.
  • Edit or delete any sighting or comment at any time, and choose per sighting whether its area is shown.
  • For anything else, write to the Grievance Officer. We may need to confirm it's you before acting.

10. Children

NatureGram is for explorers aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13: sign-up asks for a month and year of birth, refuses anyone under 13, and keeps only the answer, never the date.

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, anyone under 18 is a child. For explorers aged 13 to 17 we ask for verifiable consent from a parent or lawful guardian before their notebook can be shared. At sign-up the explorer gives a parent's email address; we send that adult a letter explaining what NatureGram is and what we keep, with a single-use link that works for 7 days. The parent signs in to their own verified, adult NatureGram account and confirms they are 18 or older and the parent or guardian. We record the consent (which account gave it, when, the version of this notice, and a one-way hash of the network address) and delete the parent's email address from the request once it is answered. Until a parent approves, the explorer can browse and keep a private journal, but cannot share sightings, write letters or appear to others.

For explorers under 18 ("teen mode") we keep the month they turn 18 instead of a date of birth, and: show no advertising and load no advertising or measurement tags; send no usage analytics (only error reports without identifiers); never show where a sighting was made; allow letters only between explorers who follow each other; leave them out of search and explorer suggestions for people who don't follow them; and instruct the AI guide never to ask for personal details. We do not track or monitor their behaviour or target them with ads. Teen mode ends automatically on the first day of the month they turn 18.

A parent or guardian who gave consent can, from Profile → Settings → Explorers in your care, withdraw it at any time (the notebook becomes private again) or ask us to delete the child's account; the account is closed at once and its data deleted within 30 days. If you believe a child under 13, or a teen without a parent's consent, has an account, write to the Grievance Officer and we will act on it.

11. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls that limit administrative access to a single named administrator protected by strong sign-in, server-side checks on every AI request and every share, and audit logs of administrative actions. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data we will notify you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

12. Grievances and complaints

Write to our Grievance Officer, Roshini Ganuthula, at roshiniganuthula@gmail.com. We acknowledge complaints within 24 hours and resolve them within 15 days. Requests to remove unlawful content are acted on within 72 hours, and content that exposes a person's private areas, shows nudity or impersonates someone is removed within 24 hours of a complaint. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India or your local data protection authority.

13. Changes

We will post any changes here with a new date, and tell you in the app before material changes take effect.
