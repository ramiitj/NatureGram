Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

NatureGram is a live AI field guide and community for people who love nature. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, username, and optional profile photo and bio.
  • Your content: photos, videos, audio, captions, comments, likes and follows you choose to make.
  • Location: if you allow it, your device location is used to name the approximate area of a sighting. Exact coordinates are never shown publicly. They are stored privately in your account for your own records. You can hide the area on any post.
  • Live guide sessions: when you use the live lens, camera frames and microphone audio are streamed to our AI provider to generate the guide's responses. We do not record or store these streams.
  • Technical data: basic logs needed to keep the service secure and reliable (for example, error reports and usage counts). We do not log your camera, audio or message content.

How we use it

  • To run NatureGram: show your posts, power the AI guide and identification, send notifications you've asked for.
  • To keep the community safe: moderation, abuse prevention and fair-use limits on AI features.
  • To improve identification accuracy: corrections you make to AI suggestions may be used, without your identity, to evaluate how well the AI performs.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use device fingerprinting.

AI processing

NatureGram uses Google's Gemini models to identify species and describe behaviour. AI suggestions can be wrong. Every AI identification is labelled as a suggestion. Never eat, touch or handle a wild organism based on an AI identification.

Who can see what

  • Posts shared to the feed are public. Journal-only posts are visible only to you.
  • Your profile (username, photo, bio, public posts, follower counts) is visible to other explorers.
  • Reports you make are visible only to moderators.

Your choices and rights

  • Download your data: Profile → Settings → Download my data.
  • Delete your account: Profile → Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, posts, comments, likes, follows and uploaded media.
  • Edit or delete any post or comment at any time, and change location sharing per post.
  • Depending on where you live (for example, under GDPR or CCPA), you may have additional rights to access, correct or object to processing. Contact us to exercise them.

Children

NatureGram is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
