Terms of Use

Last updated: November 2026

Using NatureGram

You must be at least 13 years old. Keep your account secure and do not share it. You are responsible for what you post.

Community guidelines

  • Respect wildlife: observe without disturbing, baiting or harming animals, nests or habitats.
  • Protect sensitive species: never share exact locations of threatened species, nests or dens.
  • Respect people: no harassment, hate, spam or impersonation. Do not post photos of people without consent.
  • Post only content you have the right to share.
  • No content that promotes poaching, illegal collection or trade in wildlife.

We may remove content or suspend accounts that break these guidelines. You can report posts from the ⋯ menu.

AI features

AI identifications and insights are suggestions, not expert advice, and may be inaccurate. Do not rely on them for decisions about safety, health or the edibility of any organism. Fair-use limits apply to AI features.

Your content

You keep ownership of what you post. By posting publicly you allow NatureGram to host, display and share it within the service (for example, in the feed and in share previews). You can delete your content at any time.

Disclaimer
